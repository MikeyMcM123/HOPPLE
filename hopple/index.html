About Hopple

Change one letter per move to get from the start word to the goal. Matching letters get lily pads. The frog follows your latest word along the left bank. Solve it and it hops across.

Daily puzzles have a shortest solution of 4–6 moves. There’s a new one at 00:00 UTC. For more puzzles, try Practice.

The included dictionary is a limited word list; some English words aren’t accepted. Puzzle difficulty is based on shortest route length, not vocabulary familiarity.

Privacy

This version saves puzzle progress, results, and preferences in your browser’s local storage. There are no accounts, analytics scripts, or advertising scripts included in this version.

Sharing uses your device’s share options or clipboard. Custom puzzle links contain the two words you selected.

Your hosting provider may process ordinary connection data, such as IP addresses and request logs. Deleting this site’s browser data removes saved games and settings.
